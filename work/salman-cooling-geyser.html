SALMAN COOLING & GEYSER CENTER · DESKTOP EXPERIENCE

THE PROJECT

A local service site built for quick trust and action.

Salman Cooling & Geyser Center needed a website that communicated reliability, highlighted core services, and made it easy for local customers to get in touch. The site needed to feel practical and immediate rather than overly corporate.

I designed and built the site with a mobile-first, service-oriented structure, emphasizing clarity, contact calls-to-action, and accessible layout decisions. The result supports local visibility while making it easy for customers to reach out when they need support.
