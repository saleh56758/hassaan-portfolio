ZYLVA · DESKTOP EXPERIENCE

THE PROJECT

A refined storefront for a premium jewelry brand.

Zylva needed a polished digital presence that felt elevated and trustworthy while remaining simple to browse on any device. The brief focused on a clean storefront, strong product presentation, and a smooth customer journey.

I built the site with WordPress and Elementor, paying close attention to visual hierarchy, load performance, and layout consistency across breakpoints. The outcome is a refined brand-led experience that supports product discovery without unnecessary clutter.
